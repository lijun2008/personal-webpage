03 · Research outputs

From insight
to impact.

Recent work spans supercritical water gasification, circular materials, hydrogen systems, industrial combustion, and sustainable fuel production.

SCWG

Supercritical water gasification

Validated kinetic models improve prediction of syngas yields and cold gas efficiency from wet lignocellulosic biomass.

H₂

Whisky co-product valorisation

Draff, pot ale, and spent lees are assessed as feedstocks for thermal digestion and catalyst-assisted hydrogen production.

CAT

Sustainable tar reforming

Activated and metal-impregnated chars provide cost-effective catalysts for syngas cleaning and pollutant removal.

3D

Biomass pyrolysis modelling

Three-dimensional particle models reveal how heat transfer and porosity evolution shape pyrolysis performance.
